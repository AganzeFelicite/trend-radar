What's stored

items 103 rows

  • source, item_id (key)
  • item_type: video · stream · clip · article · channel
  • title, url
  • channel_id, channel_title
  • published_at, first_seen_at

signals 464 rows

  • source, item_id → items
  • metric_name: views · likes · comments · viewers · pageviews · subscribers
  • metric_value
  • captured_at (hour or day bucket)

Unique per item, metric and bucket. That's why re-runs never duplicate.

raw_responses 29 rows

  • source, endpoint
  • fetched_at
  • payload (full JSON)

Kept 30 days (cleanup job next).

connector_runs 7 rows

  • source, job
  • started_at, finished_at
  • status: ok · error · waiting
  • items_count, quota_used, error

The quota guard sums today's quota_used.

topics · topic_items · topic_scores Phase 3

Stable topic IDs, which videos belong to each, hourly momentum score + flags.

waitlist · alerts_sent Phase 4–5

Emails (validated, rate-limited) and one alert per topic per day.

Risks and gaps

High
History lives on one laptop No server and no backups yet. If Docker stops, we lose those hours for good; if the disk dies, everything is gone. Fixed by the VPS + nightly R2 backup.
Medium
Spam tops the raw view counts #1 right now: 2.6M views, 18 comments, hashtag-only title. Scoring has to filter this (Phase 3.3).
Medium
YouTube quota climbs as videos pile up Discovery is a fixed 4,800/day; snapshots grow until the 7-day window fills (~Nov 10). Rough worst case nears the 9,000 stop. If it does: check older videos every 3h instead of every hour.
Medium
Many languages Chinese, Portuguese, Arabic, French in the top 15. Your decision: English-only or all, before topics are built.
Low
No Twitch data until the category exists Handled: the job waits and starts by itself. Likely around release day.
Low
Nobody notices if the worker dies No monitoring yet. Healthchecks.io + Sentry come with the server (P2.6).
